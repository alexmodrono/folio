## Ask about a passage

Select text in the PDF and press ⌘L. The passage waits as a chip above the message box, labeled with the document and page. Type your question and send it yourself. Folio never sends a message for you.

[Ask about a passage →](/asking/)

![Folio with a passage highlighted in a PDF about photosynthesis and ChatGPT's one-sentence explanation in the side panel](/images/screens/ask.webp)

## Ask about a whole page

When the answer depends on a figure, a table or a formula, ask about the page instead. Folio adds an image of the page, so ChatGPT sees the layout and not just the text.

[Ask about a page →](/asking/#ask-about-a-whole-page)

![A question about page 1 in the chat panel, with ChatGPT describing the page's two diagrams](/images/screens/page.webp)

## Find your questions again

Pages you asked about get a small bubble in the margin. Click it to see what you asked and jump back to that question in the chat. Bubbles are drawn by Folio and never written into your PDF.

[Question bubbles →](/questions/)

![A question bubble's popover in the page margin, showing the quoted passage, the question and a Show in Chat button](/images/screens/bubble.webp)

## A folder, not a database

Your library is a plain folder, `~/Documents/Folio` by default. Folders you make in Folio are folders in Finder. If you use [Findle](https://github.com/alexmodrono/Findle), your Moodle courses show up in the sidebar too.

[Your library →](/library/)

## A reader that stays out of the way

Single page, continuous scroll, two pages or book mode, a thumbnail strip, find and zoom. Rotating only turns the view; the file on disk stays as it was.

## ChatGPT beside the page

Folio shows chatgpt.com in a panel next to your PDF, signed in with your own ChatGPT account. Your plan, your chat history and your settings are the same ones you use in the browser.

- **Your own account.** Sign in once; Folio has no account of its own.
- **You press Send.** Folio fills the message box. Only your Return or your click sends.
- **One upload per chat.** Folio checks the conversation before attaching a PDF again.
- **Up to four chats.** Side by side or stacked, each with its own conversation.
- **Small and native.** Built with AppKit and PDFKit, with no third-party code.
- **English and Spanish.** The app follows your Mac's language.

[Sign in to ChatGPT →](/sign-in/)

## Start with one PDF

[Install Folio](/quickstart/), sign in to ChatGPT, open a PDF, and ask about one paragraph. The [troubleshooting guide](/troubleshooting/) covers sign-in, attachments, and changes on chatgpt.com.

Folio isn't affiliated with OpenAI. "ChatGPT" refers to the service you sign in to. You can [read the code](https://github.com/alexmodrono/folio) or [build it yourself](/building/).
