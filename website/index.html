Folio

A PDF reader for Mac with ChatGPT next to the page. Select a passage, ask about it, and keep reading.

Download for Mac

macOS 26 or later

What it does

  • Opens PDFs in a native reader with thumbnails, search, zoom and printing.
  • Shows chatgpt.com in a side panel, signed in with your own ChatGPT account.
  • Attaches the open PDF to the chat, and quotes selected text with its page number when you ask about it.
  • Marks the pages you asked about, so you can find the answer again later.
  • Keeps your PDFs in a plain folder in Documents. If you use Findle, your Moodle courses show up in the sidebar.

Requirements

  • A Mac with macOS 26 or later.
  • A ChatGPT account. What you can attach and send depends on your plan.

Works with your own ChatGPT account. Not affiliated with OpenAI.
